Linux has different versions called distros.

Ubuntu-->Good for beginners.

Kali Linux-->Used a lot in cybersecurity and ethical hacking.

Parrot OS-->Cybersecurity + privacy focused.

Debian-->Simple and stable.

Fedora-->modern and developer friendly.

arch-->customizable and advanced.

Ubuntu is one of the most used linux distros because it is beginner friendly and simple to use.
Kali Linux is popular in cybersecurity, while Debian is known for being stable and lightweight.
If you are starting linux today, UBUNTU is usually the best distro to begin with.
